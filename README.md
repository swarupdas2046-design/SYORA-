# SYORA 🎵

### *Feel Your Mood. Find Your Music.*

**SYORA** is an AI-powered, emotion-aware music recommendation system that detects facial expressions using a camera and suggests music based on the user's predicted mood.

Whether you're smiling, feeling low, or simply neutral, SYORA aims to connect your expressions with music that matches your moment.

**Your expressions. Your mood. Your music.** 💜
